ECHOWhy zero · Three reasons
Such an obvious gap. Why has nobody built it?
The demand is new; last-generation memory is too coarse to hold it; and doing it right is hard.
Reason 01 · Too new
This demand only exploded this year.
A year ago an agent was a personal tool; losing context only hurt you. “A team, everyone with an agent, delivering one thing together” is a 2026 pattern. The problem is under a year old — of course the product doesn’t exist yet.

Reason 02 · Granularity
Last-generation products describe the person, not the work in progress.
Their unit of memory is one sentence about you: a single block, static, no time. Work state is a chain of steps, each with an owner, a time and a reason. The granularity is off by an order of magnitude — not unwilling, just unable to hold it.

Reason 03 · Hard
It has to be small and keep every detail, at once.
Compress by summarizing and the detail is gone before you know it mattered; keep everything and tokens explode. The only way through is to compress work into structured state at the moment it is written. That is the genuinely hard part — and Echo’s core technology.
