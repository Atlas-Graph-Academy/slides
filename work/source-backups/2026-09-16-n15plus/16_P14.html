ECHOWhy we exist
Every new medium has pushed civilization forward.
In the agent era, Echo is the medium of thought.

Writing preserved language; images preserved faces; video let a moment exist apart from the room it happened in.

Thought never had a medium like that. A person’s judgment, memory and intent usually vanish the moment they leave.

Today, Echo preserves how a thought was formed: who judged, on what basis, wanting what. Even when the person is absent, the thought can be understood, used and remade.

Every era gives birth to its own economy. The agent era will too. In that economy, a company’s context is its most important asset, and it becomes a commodity that trades and flows fast. People get freer for it.

So the work we do today is heavy. Underneath it, we are building the knowledge engine for that society.
